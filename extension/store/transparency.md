# QuickCopy transparency statement — version 0.1.1

## What QuickCopy is

QuickCopy Wallet Control is an independent, local browser extension for choosing a previously saved **public** Ethereum or Solana address while preparing a direct reply on X. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to X, Twitter, Ethereum, Solana, or a wallet provider.

## When it appears

QuickCopy runs only on `x.com` and `twitter.com`. It adds its own labeled picker only to posts that contain direct public-address-request phrasing, such as “Drop your SOL address,” “Comment your wallet,” or “Paste your address.” It does not appear on general posts that merely mention cryptocurrency, Ethereum, Solana, mints, claims, or market activity.

## What happens after a click

The user opens the clearly labeled QuickCopy control and deliberately selects one of their saved public addresses. The extension copies that address and may insert it into the reply draft. The user reviews the draft and manually presses X’s final Post button. QuickCopy never performs the final post action.

## What QuickCopy does not do

QuickCopy does not ask for, receive, use, store, or transmit seed phrases, recovery phrases, private keys, passwords, login credentials, wallet connections, transaction approvals, signatures, or payments. It does not use analytics, external APIs, remote code, affiliate links, or advertising. It does not change post text, redirect a user, or alter X settings.

## Data and permissions

Wallet labels, public addresses, order, theme, and display settings are stored only in local extension storage. `clipboardWrite` is used only after the user selects an address. X host access is used only to identify direct address requests, show the QuickCopy picker, and insert the user-selected address in a reply draft. Firefox’s manifest declares no data collection or transmission.
